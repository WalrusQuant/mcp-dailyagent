What's in it

A durable store for the life stuff you already track

Tasks, habits, journal, workouts, focus sessions, goals. All in Postgres, all behind one typed API.

TASK Tasks Franklin Covey A1–C9 priority, rollover, recurrence, space + goal linking. ∞ depth
HABIT Habits Daily or weekly, ISO-weekday target days, streak tracking via a dead-simple log table. 7d / 52w
JRNL Journal One entry per day, mood 1–5, full-text search across everything you've ever written. 365/y
WORK Workouts Templates and logged sessions. Strength, timed, or cardio. Per-exercise sets, reps, weight. per-set
FOCUS Focus sessions Pomodoro timer data, optionally linked to a task. 1–480 min duration, 0–120 min break. 1–480m
GOAL Goals Categories, progress %, target dates, a progress log table for trend lines. 0–100%
AREA Spaces Projects / areas of life that group tasks, habits, and goals under a single umbrella. nested
BRIEF Daily briefings Written by OpenClaw via save_daily_briefing. Displayed read-only on the dashboard. read-only
REVW Weekly reviews Written by OpenClaw via save_weekly_review. Displayed read-only on the dashboard. read-only
